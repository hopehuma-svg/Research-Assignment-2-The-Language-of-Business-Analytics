# BrightLearn Business Analytics: Module 01 - Terminology Assignment

This repository contains my submission for the research assignment "The Language of Business Analytics" under the Business Analytics Foundations course. 

## Project Objective

The goal of this assignment is to research, analyze, and define 38 core terms, metrics, and key performance indicators (KPIs) fundamental to data-driven business decisions. The framework focuses on distinguishing overlapping concepts (such as metrics versus KPIs or data analytics versus business intelligence) and applying them to industry environments.

### Research Framework
For every single term, the assignment includes:
* A brief, original definition written in plain language.
* A practical industry example showcasing real-world business application.
* Complete citations using credible corporate and academic references.

## Core Content Structure

The project file is organized into four main operational areas:
* Foundational Concepts: Broad definitions covering analytics disciplines and management philosophies.
* Data & Reporting: Concepts detailing database infrastructure, metric structures, and reporting dashboards.
* Sales & Customer KPIs: Performance measures focusing on user lifecycles, acquisition costs, and customer value.
* Marketing Analytics: Criteria evaluating marketing campaign attribution, optimization metrics, and continuous testing frameworks.

## Repository Contents

* /research-document.pdf - The complete submission file containing all 38 documented terms.
* /references.txt - A bibliography detailing the minimum five baseline industry sources utilized for this research (including Harvard Business Review, McKinsey, and Investopedia).

## Academic Integrity Reference

All definitions, commentary, and business cases were written independently to ensure accurate conceptual mastery and total compliance with academic standards.
